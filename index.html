How I handle every request

Whether it's a training registration, a supplier query or a manager's ad hoc ask, I follow the same path from first contact to closed file.

  1. Log it

    Record the request the moment it arrives, with the owner and the SLA it has to meet.

  2. Check the documents

    Confirm every detail and attachment is complete and correct before anything moves.

  3. Process it

    Follow the SOP and work instructions for that account, escalating early if a deadline is at risk.

  4. Update the record

    Keep the file accurate, confidential and easy to retrieve at any point in its life cycle.

  5. Report and improve

    Roll it into weekly and monthly reports, and simplify any step that keeps slowing the team down.
